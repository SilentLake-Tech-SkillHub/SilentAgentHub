# Product Research

## Workflow

1. Define the research question, decision it informs, freshness requirement, and evidence standard.
2. Inspect local product and implementation state first.
3. Use official documentation, primary sources, live product pages, browser interaction, or connected data when facts may have changed.
4. Record source, access date, finding, confidence, contradiction, and implication. Distinguish observation from inference.
5. Cover user experience, business effect, system mechanism, upstream and downstream relationships, risks, and open facts.
6. Write results to `产品管理/研究记录.md` or the routed research artifact, then update the requirement, decision, Plan, and risk record affected by the findings.

Do not deliver a source dump. Deliver the smallest evidence-backed set of findings that changes a product or engineering decision.
